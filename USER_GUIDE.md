# How to Use the Birthday Card Maker

## Creating a Birthday Card

1. **Open the app.** You'll see a simple form on a cream-colored background.

2. **Type the recipient's name** in the "Recipient Name" box. This is the person you're making the card for.

3. **Write your birthday message** in the message box below it. Write anything you like — the box will count your characters so you know how much space you have left (up to 500 characters).

4. **Add photos** (optional but fun!):
   - Tap or click the photo upload area.
   - Choose up to 6 photos from your device.
   - Each photo will show up as a small preview.
   - Don't want a photo anymore? Tap the little "x" on its corner to remove it.

5. **Check for errors.** If you missed a required field, you'll see a short message in orange-red text telling you what to fix.

6. **Tap the "Create Card" button** at the bottom of the screen.
   - The button will show "Creating..." while your card is being made.
   - Please wait and don't tap it again during this time.

7. **You're done!** You'll be taken straight to your new birthday card page.

---

## Sharing Your Card

1. On your card page, look for the **"Share" button** (it floats near the bottom of the screen).

2. Tap it. This will either:
   - Copy the card's link to your clipboard (you'll see a "Link copied!" message), or
   - Open your phone's share menu so you can send it directly via text, email, or an app.

3. Paste or send the link to the person you want to share it with — anyone with the link can view the card.

---

## Viewing a Card

- **Photos:** Tap any photo in the gallery to see it bigger. On phones, swipe left or right to see the next or previous photo. Tap outside the photo to close it.
- **Message:** Your written message appears in a card-style box, keeping your original line breaks.

---

## If Something Goes Wrong

- **"Card not found":** This means the link is broken or the card no longer exists. Tap the button on screen to create a new card.
- **404 / Page Not Found:** If you land on a page that doesn't exist, you'll see a friendly message and a button to start creating a new card.

---

## Good to Know

- You don't need to create an account or log in.
- Each link only leads to **one** card — there's no dashboard or list of cards to manage.
- There are no payments or purchases involved — the app is completely free to use.
- Once you create a card and share the link, save that link somewhere safe (like your notes or messages) — it's the only way to find that card again.
