Birthday Wishes Card

A prototype walkthrough — how the site will work for you and the people you send cards to.

What it is: a simple website for making a personalized birthday card for someone — their name, a message from you, and a few photos of your memories together. It creates a private link you can text or share, and it opens nicely on a phone, tablet, or computer.

  • No sign-up or account needed — just fill in the form and share the link.
  • Works from any device — Android, iPhone, or a regular computer browser.
  • Each card gets its own private, shareable link.

1 Create the card

You open the site, type in the recipient's name, write your birthday message, and add a few photos — memories, selfies together, anything you like.

The create-card form

2 Get your shareable link

As soon as you hit "Create Card," the site generates a private link for that card and shows it to you — ready to copy or share directly.

Your link is ready to copy or share

3 They open the card

Whoever you send the link to just taps it — no app, no login. They see the name, your message, and the photos in a warm, celebratory layout. It looks just as good on a laptop as it does on a phone.
