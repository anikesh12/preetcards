Birthday Wishes Card — V2

What's new since v1: more occasions, more ways to show your memories, and the first monetization pieces.

1 Any occasion, not just birthdays

The creation form now starts with an "Occasion" dropdown — Birthday, Anniversary, Wedding, Engagement, Congratulations, New Baby, Get Well Soon, Farewell, Retirement, or Thank You. Whichever one you pick, the card page's heading adapts automatically (e.g. "Happy Anniversary, Meera & Raj!").

Occasion dropdown, top of the form

2 Choose how your photos are shown

Below the photo upload, pick from 4 collage styles for how the memory photos appear on the card: Grid (classic even layout), Spotlight (one featured photo + smaller ones below), Filmstrip (horizontal scroll), and Scatter (polaroid-style, slightly rotated — shown in the example below).

4 collage styles to choose from

3 Download a memory (with a small watermark)

Each photo on the card page now has a small download button. Downloaded photos carry a subtle "Birthday Wishes 🎉" watermark in the corner — a "Remove Watermark" option is visible for people who want a clean copy (payment for that isn't wired up yet — see the note below).

Scatter collage + download button + ad space (see below)
